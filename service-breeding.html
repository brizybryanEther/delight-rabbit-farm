Genetic Superiority

True-to-Type Purebred Rabbits with Documented Lineage

The foundation of a profitable rabbit farm lies in its genetics. Unchecked cross-breeding leads to smaller litters, slow weight gain, high kit mortality, and poor feed conversion.

At Delight Rabbit Farm, our purebred breeding stock is selectively bred across generations for vigor, high mothering capability, rapid feed conversion, and heavy bone-to-meat ratios. Every breeding animal undergoes physical veterinary inspection, deworming, and quarantine before handover.

  • Verified Lineage Records: Ancestry tracking to guarantee zero inbreeding depression.
  • Complete Health Guarantee: 100% vaccinated, dewormed, and health-inspected before dispatch.
  • High Litter Fecundity: Proven dam lines delivering an average of 8-12 healthy kits per litter.
  • Starter Support & Acclimatization Guide: Best feeding and housing advice to settle your new stock smoothly.

Available Purebred Breeds

Choose from world-class commercial meat and breeding breeds acclimated to Rwanda's climate.

New Zealand White

Top Meat Breed

The international gold standard for commercial meat farming. Unmatched growth rates, dense loin muscling, calm temperament, and prolific litters (9-12 kits).

Mature Weight: 4.5 – 5.5 kg
Best For: Commercial Meat & Breeding

Californian White

High Dressing %

Distinctive dark points on ears, nose, and feet. Renowned for broad shoulders, muscular hindquarters, very high bone-to-meat ratio, and excellent maternal instincts.

Mature Weight: 4.0 – 5.0 kg
Best For: Commercial Meat & Crossbreeding

Flemish Giant

The Gentle Giant

The largest domestic rabbit breed in the world. Exceptional frame, docile personality, and massive bone structure. Ideal for upgrading the carcass size of smaller herds.

Mature Weight: 6.5 – 9.0+ kg
Best For: Herd Size Upgrading & Pets

Checkered Giant

Hardy & Prolific

Athletic, muscular, and alert with handsome butterfly markings. Long arched back with deep loins, highly vigorous, active foragers that thrive on diverse feed.

Mature Weight: 5.0 – 6.5 kg
Best For: Hardy Breeding & Meat

Dutch Rabbit

Compact & Robust

Famous two-toned pattern with white blaze and saddle. Exceptionally hardy, low feed requirement, highly docile, outstanding foster mothers for orphan kits.

Mature Weight: 2.2 – 3.0 kg
Best For: Smallholder Farms, Fostering & Pets
