The Vital Role of Roughage in Preventing Disease

In rabbit physiology, fiber is not merely a supplement—it is the lifeline of their entire digestive tract. Without sufficient long-strand fiber, rabbits quickly fall victim to fatal Gastrointestinal (GI) stasis, bloating, and hairball blockages.

At Delight Rabbit Farm, our hay is harvested at prime vegetative stages, carefully sun-cured to reduce moisture below 12%, and packed into compact bales. This preserves essential chlorophyll and sweet natural aroma while preventing dangerous mold and dust.

  • Cures GI Stasis: Long coarse fiber keeps the stomach and intestines moving 24/7.
  • Natural Tooth Wear: Abrasive silica fibers wear down constantly growing rabbit incisors and molars.
  • Dust & Mold Free: Properly cured and dry-stored to protect sensitive rabbit respiratory airways.
  • Multiple Grass Varieties: Rhodes grass, Timothy grass, and high-calcium alfalfa for all life stages.

Our Hay Varieties

Which Hay is Right for Your Herd?

Different stages of a rabbit's life require tailored mineral and protein concentrations.

Rhodes Grass Hay

Ideal Everyday Roughage

Sweet, palatable perennial grass with high digestible fiber and moderate protein. Perfect unlimited daily roughage for adult bucks, resting does, and fatteners.

  • Maintains ideal digestive pH
  • Low calcium prevents bladder sludge
  • Available in compact 12-15kg bales

Alfalfa (Lucerne) Hay

High Calcium & Protein

Rich legume hay packed with protein (16-19%) and calcium. Essential for rapid bone development in kits under 6 months and supporting milk supply in nursing does.

  • Greatly boosts nursing doe milk yield
  • Accelerates kit bone & muscle growth
  • Highly aromatic and palatable

Wilted Forage Bundles

Safe Fresh Greens

Specially harvested and pre-wilted cuttings of Desmodium, Sweet Potato vines, and Calliandra from our dedicated, chemical-free forage plots.

  • Pre-wilted to eliminate excess moisture & gas
  • Zero roadside dust or exhaust contamination
  • Provides natural vitamins & hydration

How We Harvest & Cure Our Hay

01

Prime Cut

Grass is harvested early in the morning before flowering to capture the highest concentration of leaf nutrients.

02

Sun Curing

Evenly spread on clean raised drying tarps, turned regularly under the African sun until moisture drops below 12%.

03

Tight Baling

Compressed into uniform 12-15 kg rectangular bales tied with strong twine, easy to stack and transport in any vehicle.

04

Palletized Storage

Stored in dry, shaded, ventilated barns on elevated wooden pallets to ensure zero moisture seepage or mold formation.
