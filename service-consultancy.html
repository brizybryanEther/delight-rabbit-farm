Turnkey Engineering

Avoid Costly Infrastructure Mistakes Before You Build

Poor hutch ventilation, inappropriate wire mesh sizes, improper waste drainage, and inadequate predator protection cause over 80% of rabbit farm failures in Rwanda.

Our consultancy team combines ten years of commercial farming experience with practical architectural and veterinary knowledge. We design ergonomic, hygienic, multi-tier galvanized cage systems with automated nipple watering lines and separate urine-slurry drainage that make daily chores effortless while keeping rabbits pristine and odor-free.

  • Custom Architectural Blueprints: Exact 2D/3D layouts tailored to your land dimensions and budget.
  • Automated Watering Systems: Self-cleaning stainless-steel nipple lines that prevent water contamination.
  • Slurry & Manure Separation: Slanted drop-trays that direct urine to collection barrels for organic fertilizer.
  • Financial Feasibility Model: 1-year to 3-year cash flow projections, feed budgets, and ROI timeline.

Comprehensive Scope

Our 5 Core Advisory Pillars

We guide you through every milestone from empty plot to a fully populated, thriving rabbitry.

1. Site Selection & Shed Orientation

Evaluating prevailing wind directions, solar angles, slope drainage, natural tree shade, and road accessibility to build an optimal micro-climate that prevents heat stress.

2. Hutch Blueprints & BOQ

Detailed material specifications, galvanized wire gauges, rust-proof framing, self-cleaning dropping pans, and automated nipple drinking line plumbing diagrams.

3. Biosecurity Architecture

Sanitary footbath entryways, designated isolation/quarantine pens, predator-proof perimeter meshing, and vector control protocols against wild rodents and insects.

4. Herd Records & Software

Standardized doe breeding cards, mating calendars, ear tagging schemes, mortality trackers, and daily feed budgeting templates for flawless farm audits.

5. Commercial Feasibility

Realistic capital expenditure (CAPEX) vs. operational expenditure (OPEX) calculations, breakeven milestones, and market linkages for selling purebred stock and meat.

6. Waste Monetization

Transforming rabbit manure and urine into high-value organic liquid foliar fertilizer and worm composting vermicompost for coffee and horticulture growers.
