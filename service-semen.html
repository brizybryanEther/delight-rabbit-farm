Elite Genetics for High-Efficiency Commercial Herds

Artificial Insemination (AI) is the modern standard for commercial rabbit farming worldwide. It enables farmers to rapidly multiply elite genetics across dozens of does simultaneously while maintaining flawless biosecurity.

Our donor bucks are champion specimens of New Zealand White, Californian White, and Flemish Giant breeds—selected for superior growth velocity, broad carcass width, high dressing percentage, and disease resistance. Semen is collected, evaluated under microscopy, extended with high-viability protective diluents, and packed in temperature-controlled transport kits.

  • Microscopically Tested: Minimum 85% progressive sperm motility guaranteed per dose.
  • Biosecurity Perfection: Eliminates transmission of venereal diseases and cage injuries.
  • Synchronized Batch Kindling: Inseminate 20+ does in a single morning for synchronized meat harvesting.
  • Cost Efficiency: Access premium stud genetics without the ongoing feed and housing cost of keeping bucks.

Why Artificial Insemination?

Key Benefits of Rabbit AI for Your Farm

Modern commercial rabbitries rely on AI to scale output and maintain superior herd genetics.

Major Cost Savings

Eliminate the cost of feeding, watering, and housing aggressive mature bucks that only mate a few times per month.

Zero Disease Transfer

Completely stops venereal spirochetosis (rabbit syphilis), snuffles spread, and physical fighting wounds between animals during mating.

Batch Kindling Control

Breed all your ready does within a 2-hour window. Litters kindle on the exact same date for streamlined feeding, weaning, and market harvesting.

Accelerated Upgrading

Introduce top-ranked international genetic lines to local commercial does, immediately increasing average offspring slaughter weights.

Step-by-Step Workflow

How the AI Process Operates

01

Doe Synchronization

Check does for estrus (reddish-purple vulva) and synchronize breeding batches using our recommended lighting/feed stimulation protocol.

02

Semen Preparation

We collect from certified purebred bucks, analyze sperm motility, mix with high-grade extender, and dispatch in cold packs.

03

Insemination

Apply doses using disposable bent-tip AI pipettes and induce ovulation using GnRH protocol. Done in seconds with zero doe stress.

04

Pregnancy Check

Palpate does at 10-14 days to confirm high conception rates (>80%). Prepare nest boxes at day 28 for synchronized kindling.
