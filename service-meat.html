The Super-Meat

Lean, Nutritious & Deliciously Tender

Rabbit meat is globally recognized by doctors and nutritionists as the healthiest domestic meat available. Naturally low in fat, ultra-low in cholesterol, and containing more protein per gram than beef or chicken.

At Delight Rabbit Farm, our meat rabbits are raised under strict hygienic welfare standards, fed balanced pellets and pesticide-free Rhodes grass hay. Harvested at the prime age of 12-14 weeks, our meat delivers exceptional tenderness with a mild, succulent flavor that pairs wonderfully with Rwandan and international cuisine.

  • Highest Lean Protein: Over 21% digestible protein to fuel vitality and muscle recovery.
  • Cardio-Friendly: Lowest cholesterol of all commonly consumed domestic meats.
  • Hygienically Dressed: Inspected, thoroughly washed, and vacuum-sealed in cold-chain packs.
  • Reliable Supply: Standing weekly orders available for hotels, restaurants, and butcheries in Kigali.
