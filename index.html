About Us

A Passionate Purebred Rabbit Farm in Rwanda

Breeding purebred rabbits with verified pedigree and health-checked stock for Rwandan farmers.

At Delight Rabbit Farm, we specialise in purebred rabbits with documented pedigrees and certified quality genetics. Our farm in Rwanda focuses on breed purity, selective breeding, and meticulous health and record keeping so every rabbit we supply is healthy, productive, and true to its line.

Purebred, Pedigreed Stock
Every rabbit comes with documented pedigree and breed information so you know exactly what you are buying.
Farmer-First Support
We provide training, guidance, and ongoing support so your rabbit farming venture succeeds from day one.

Our Services

Purebred Rabbit Farming Services

01

Rabbit Farming Training

Hands-on courses for beginners and commercial farmers covering housing, feeding, breeding, health management, and business planning.

02

Breeding Rabbits

Purebred breeding stock with breed and pedigree information on request. Bucks, does, and young stock available.

03

Rabbit Semen

Semen from purebred bucks to improve herd genetics and keep breed lines pure. Handled and delivered with care.

04

Rabbit Meat

Fresh, lean, hygienically processed rabbit meat from healthy, well-managed stock. For households, restaurants, and retailers.

05

Rabbit Pellets

Balanced, nutritious feed pellets formulated for every growth stage of rabbits.

06

Hay & Forage

Quality hay and fresh forage for healthy digestion and optimal rabbit health.

07

Farm Consultancy

Farm setup, hutch design, herd management, and profitability advice for new and existing rabbit farms.
