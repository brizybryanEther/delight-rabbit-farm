Precision Nutrition

Formulated for Superior Growth & Digestive Health

Feeding rabbits unformulated mash or uncontrolled food scraps causes bloat, enteritis, and uneven growth. Our commercial rabbit pellets are engineered to deliver the precise balance of amino acids, crude fiber, vitamins, and minerals that rabbits need to thrive.

Manufactured with premium milled grains, dehydrated alfalfa meal, oilseed proteins, and mineral premixes, our pellets are uniformly compressed at optimal hardness to ensure teeth wear naturally while eliminating selective feeding waste.

  • Balanced Crude Protein (16-18%): Promotes lean muscle development without kidney strain.
  • High Digestible Fiber (15-18%): Prevents fatal GI stasis and maintains healthy cecal fermentation.
  • Zero Feed Wastage: Solid, dust-free cylindrical pellets that rabbits consume cleanly.
  • Convenient Sizes: Available in 10 kg, 25 kg, and 50 kg heavy-duty protective sacks.

Formulated Formulas

Feed Blends for Every Production Stage

Choose the exact nutritional profile matching your herd's current stage of growth or reproduction.

Grower & Fattener

17% Crude Protein

Formulated specifically for weaned kits from 6 weeks to 14 weeks. Rapidly builds muscular frames and achieves 2.5kg slaughter target within record time.

  • High lysine and methionine
  • Optimized Feed Conversion Ratio (FCR)
  • Supports dense muscle deposition

Breeder & Lactation

18% Crude Protein

Formulated for pregnant does, nursing mothers with large litters (8-12 kits), and active working stud bucks. Rich in calcium and energy.

  • High calcium (1.2%) & phosphorus for lactation
  • Prevents doe weight loss during lactation
  • Promotes vigorous sperm viability in bucks

Adult Maintenance

15% Crude Protein

Designed for resting does between breeding cycles and mature adult stock. High fiber maintains gut motility without causing unhealthy obesity.

  • High fiber (18%) prevents GI slowdown
  • Keeps does in ideal reproductive weight
  • Economical maintenance ration
