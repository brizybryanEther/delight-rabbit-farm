Course Overview

From Hobby to High-Yield Commercial Production

At Delight Rabbit Farm, we believe knowledge is the single most valuable asset in modern livestock farming. Our intensive training modules bridge the gap between basic backyard rearing and high-efficiency commercial cuniculture.

Participants gain direct, hands-on experience handling purebred rabbits, formulating balanced feed rations, designing hygienic biosecure hutches, managing breeding schedules, and keeping accurate financial records for maximum return on investment.

  • Hands-on Live Demos: Practical handling, sexing, palpation for pregnancy, and health inspections.
  • Printed Training Guide: Take-home manual with feeding charts, breeding schedules, and medication protocols.
  • 3-Month Mentorship: Direct WhatsApp mentorship and advisory as you set up or scale your farm.
  • Certificate of Completion: Recognized proof of practical cuniculture proficiency.

Comprehensive Curriculum

What You Will Learn

Every module is taught through step-by-step practical demonstration and interactive Q&A sessions.

Module 1: Housing & Infrastructure

Hutch architecture, multi-tier galvanized wire systems, natural ventilation, orientation against winds, and automated nipple drinking line installation.

Module 2: Nutrition & Feeding

Commercial pellet compounding, hay curing, safe green forages, avoiding bloat and toxic weeds, daily ration calculation, and feed-to-weight conversion.

Module 3: Breeding & Kindling

Breeding stock selection, heat detection, safe mating, nest box management, kindle monitoring, kit survival, cross-fostering, and weaning protocols.

Module 4: Health & Biosecurity

Preventing coccidiosis, snuffles (Pasteurellosis), ear mites, and GI stasis. Quarantine practices, sanitation schedules, and farm biosecurity barriers.

Module 5: Farm Economics

Cost modeling, record keeping, feed budgeting, pricing breeding stock vs. meat, marketing to restaurants, hotels, and retail meat consumers.

Module 6: Offtake & Value Addition

Hygienic slaughtering and processing techniques, pelt handling, organic rabbit manure packaging, and connecting with Kigali commercial offtakers.
